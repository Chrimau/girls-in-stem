About Us

We support women in STEM fields through mentorship and resources.


Umumazi Orizu Maurice Foundation (UOF) is a non-profit organization that aims to increase the number of women and girls in STEM fields.


At UOF, we believe that no dream is too big and no girl should be left behind. We are dedicated to empowering girls to become global leaders through STEM education and are committed to breaking barriers in science, technology, engineering, and mathematics. Our mission? Empowering young girls to lead, innovate, and break barriers in STEM fields—science, technology, engineering, and mathematics. In a world where women remain underrepresented in these industries, we are creating real opportunities to change that. Through mentorship, hands-on training, and leadership programs, we are shaping a generation of bold, confident, and skilled young women ready to lead on a global stage. Our mission is to provide a safe and supportive environment for girls to explore their interests and pursue their passions in STEM fields. We are dedicated to creating an inclusive environment where girls can thrive and succeed.


We believe that by fostering a supportive and inclusive environment, we can create a more diverse and inclusive society. We are committed to providing resources and support to girls in STEM fields, including mentorship, guidance, and opportunities for learning and growth.

Our Vision A world where girls are leaders in STEM, using their voices to shape the future. Our Mission We inspire and equip young girls with the skills, confidence, and opportunities they need to thrive in STEM and beyond.

Impact Stories

Sarah Onwunatujo

Meet Sarah, a passionate young woman who always dreamed of becoming an engineer. Growing up in a small town, she faced many challenges, including limited access to resources and mentorship.

In high school, Sarah joined our NGO’s mentorship program, where she was paired with a professional engineer who provided guidance, support, and encouragement. Through workshops and networking events, Sarah gained the skills and confidence she needed to pursue her dreams.
After completing the program, Sarah applied to several engineering colleges and was accepted into her top choice! Today, she is thriving in her studies, actively participating in student organizations, and inspiring her peers.

Sarah’s story is just one example of how our program is changing lives and empowering women to excel in STEM fields. With continued support, we believe she will make a significant impact in the engineering world.
